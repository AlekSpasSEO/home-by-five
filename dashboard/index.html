MaidThis network, measured from outside

Every number on this page comes from public sources: the search index, Google Maps packs and Google Ads keyword data, pulled on 26 August 2026. Nothing here depends on MaidThis internal analytics, which is the point. This is what an outsider can already see about the network, before anyone shares a dashboard login.

What a red or empty cell here does not mean

A market with no Google Business Profile in the top 20 Maps results is not necessarily a failing franchise. It may be awarded but not yet launched, trading under a different profile name, or ranking outside the city centroid this scan used. Treat every one as a question to ask, not a verdict.
